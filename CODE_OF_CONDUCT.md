# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of the **SUSI** open-source community pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free, constructive, and welcoming experience for everyone—across both human engineers and autonomous agent operators—regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy technical community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy, technical rigor, and kindness toward other contributors
* Grounding technical discussions in reproducible evidence, benchmarks, and test receipts
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code review and architectural feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best for the overall open-source ecosystem and community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, credentials, or API keys without explicit permission
* Submitting malicious code, backdoors, or intentional credential-exfiltration paths
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional technical setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, documentation, and extension registries) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or its community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub security/maintainer contact on [`intellibitz/susi`](https://github.com/intellibitz/susi). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
