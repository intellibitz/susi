# Susi agent instructions

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before editing. It is the canonical active engineering
contract; do not duplicate its rules or append historical mandates here.

Agents own authorized development, verification, integration and production
qualification. Requirements are not claims of current compliance. Use isolated
worktrees and private test state, preserve capabilities through working standard
sockets, and keep operational history outside source.

Core is Apache-2.0 and published one way; everyone is free to fork under the
license. The designated publisher alone holds push authority. Authorized agents
may exercise that authority within their scope; this grants no additional write
access. Follow AGENTS.md for publication, licensing, acceptance and rollback.
